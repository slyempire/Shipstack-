# Shipstack Marketing & Visual Guidance

This guide outlines the strategy for building credibility and conversion for Shipstack, specifically addressing the challenge of being a new product with limited referral proof.

## 1. Visual Theme: "Frontier Architecture"

Instead of generic tech stock photos, Shipstack's visual identity should lean into **Architectural Precision** and **Local Reliability**.

### Recommended Visuals
- **Stylized Dashboards**: High-fidelity mockups of the "Console" showing real African geography (e.g., a route map over Nairobi or Lagos). Use "Glassmorphism" and dark navy/slate themes to imply premium engineering.
- **Node Diagrams**: Abstract representations of logistics hubs connected by pulsing data lines. This reinforces the "Operating System" metaphor.
- **Workflow Snapshots**: Simple, clean UI cards showing specific actions: "Driver Verified", "Settlement Initiated", "Route Optimized".
- **Real-world Context-lite**: High-quality photography of logistics infrastructure (ports, warehouses, modern fleets) but treated with a subtle brand overlay (color tint or grain) to make them feel like "Shipstack-enabled" environments.

### What to Avoid
- **Generic "Happy Person with Tablet"**: These feel like stock photos and dilute the "Deep Tech" feel of the platform.
- **Consumer-style Illustrations**: Avoid overly rounded, playful "blob" illustrations. Shipstack is an enterprise tool; stick to sharp lines, geometric patterns, and technical isometric renders.
- **Generic Global Logos**: Do not use "fake" version of famous logos. It's better to show no logos than fake ones.

## 2. Iconography: "Industrial Tech"

Icons should be functional and consistent.

- **Library**: Use `Lucide` (which is already integrated).
- **Style**: Use `strokeWidth={2}` or `{2.5}` for a more "designed" look. Avoid solid/filled icons unless for active states.
- **Keywords**:
    - `Truck`, `Box`, `Layers`, `Activity` (for telematics).
    - `Globe`, `MapPin` (for visibility).
    - `ShieldCheck`, `Lock` (for security).
    - `Zap` (for speed/automation).
    - `CreditCard`, `Wallet` (for payouts).

## 3. Trust Signals for New Products

When you don't have 100+ testimonials, use "Verification of Capability" instead of "Verification by Volume".

- **Pilot Program Framing**: Instead of "Buy Now", use "Request Early Access" or "Join the Partner Cohort". This implies selectivity and high-touch support.
- **Integration Badges**: Highlight the "Ecosystem" you sit within. Badges for **M-Pesa**, **Flutterwave**, **Frappe**, and **ISO Standards** transfer their established trust to your brand.
- **Process Transparency**: Show a "How it Works" section with technical steps. Professional operators trust a system they can understand.
- **"Designed with Operators"**: Use this phrase to signal that even if the software is new, the *logic* behind it is battle-tested.
